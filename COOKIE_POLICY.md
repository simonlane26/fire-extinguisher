## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites:
- Remember your preferences
- Understand how you use the site
- Provide a better user experience

**Types of Cookies:**
- **Session Cookies:** Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent Cookies:** Remain on your device for a set period
- **First-Party Cookies:** Set by the website you're visiting
- **Third-Party Cookies:** Set by external services

## 7. Managing Cookies

### 7.1 Browser Controls

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

**Chrome:**
1. Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
2. Choose: Block all cookies, Block third-party, Allow all

**Firefox:**
1. Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
2. Choose: Block all, Block third-party, Allow all

**Safari:**
1. Preferences → Privacy
2. Choose: Block all cookies, Prevent cross-site tracking

**Edge:**
1. Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
2. Choose: Block all, Block third-party

**Mobile Browsers:**
- iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Block Cookies
- Chrome Mobile: Settings → Site settings → Cookies

### 7.2 Our Cookie Preferences

You can manage optional cookies in your account settings:

**Account Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences**

- ✅ Essential Cookies (Cannot be disabled)
- ⬜ Functional Cookies (Enable/Disable)
- ⬜ Analytics Cookies (Enable/Disable)

### 7.3 Impact of Disabling Cookies

**Essential Cookies Blocked:**
- You cannot log in
- Service will not function
- Security features disabled

**Functional Cookies Blocked:**
- Preferences not saved
- Reset settings each visit
- Reduced user experience

**Analytics Cookies Blocked:**
- No impact on functionality
- Helps us improve less effectively

## 8. Do Not Track (DNT)

- Essential and functional cookies still required
- No behavioral tracking occurs

**Enable DNT:**
