🔥About IgnisTech

IgnisTech is a next-generation technology company developing intelligent digital solutions for the fire and rescue sector. Our mission is to modernise the way organisations manage fire safety through automation, data-driven insights, and user-centred design.

We combine deep operational fire-service experience with advanced software engineering to create systems that are fast, scalable, and built for real-world demands. From AI-supported inspection tools to advanced asset tracking and analytics, every IgnisTech product is engineered to raise compliance standards and simplify complex processes.

As the parent company behind several innovative fire-safety platforms—including FirexCheck.com, our flagship fire extinguisher recording and compliance management system—IgnisTech is committed to reshaping the future of digital fire safety.

We don't just build software.
We build smarter, safer ways of working.
